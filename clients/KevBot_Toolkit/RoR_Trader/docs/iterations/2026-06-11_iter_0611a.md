## Root causes addressed

1. **WS coverage gap (~14% of RTH 10s windows, SPY).** The live engine NEVER inserted
   WS-missed bars; the REST verifier only corrects values of bars already in history
   ("bar not in history — skipping", ralph_engine ~3057 pre-change). Incremental
   indicators ran over a sparser series than backtest → state drift → phantom/missed/late.
2. **Lens faithfulness bug (found this morning).** `apply_rest_correction` upserts the
   corrected bar with `source='rest_correction'`, but the alert lens / `cache_locked`
   filters were `['ws','ws_agg']` — every REST-corrected bar VANISHED from the lens and
   from the 2m-gate resample. The engine consumed those bars; the lens lied by omission.
